Before you submit

Guidelines

A statement is only useful to the Audit Committee if it reflects the real position — follow these before you submit.

1

Select your SBU accurately

Each form covers the whole Group, so responses are grouped by the SBU you pick — the wrong selection lands your position in another unit's return.

2

One submission per SBU, per category

Nominate a single owner for each statement type so there's no duplicate or conflicting submission.

3

Disclose exceptions, don't just tick "compliant"

Flag control gaps, overdue items or pending remediation with a short note — that's what the Committee actually needs to see.

4

Keep your evidence on file locally

The form captures your position, not the underlying evidence. Retain supporting documents at SBU level in case of an audit query.

5

Submit before the window closes

A late submission holds up consolidation for every other SBU, not just yours.

We're here to help

Support

Common questions from SBU teams — and how to reach us for anything else.

Is there a separate financial form for my SBU?

No — one financial form now covers every SBU, including dormant entities and overseas operations. Open it and select your SBU inside the form. If your SBU isn't listed there, contact Group Risk & Control before submitting.

Do I need to submit all four statements?

Yes — financial, operational, HR and IT are usually owned by different people within your SBU, so most SBUs have four separate submissions, not one person submitting all four.

What happens after I submit?

Nothing further from you — responses are consolidated automatically into the Group reports tabled to the Audit Committee. No need to compile or forward anything yourself.

I made a mistake after submitting — what do I do?

Contact Group Risk & Control directly rather than submitting again, so the correction is reflected before consolidation.

Still stuck?

Reach the Group Risk & Control team directly and we'll sort it out.
